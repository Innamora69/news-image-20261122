newspaper front page. Lead: COP31 ends with no agreement as the EU rejects
the final draft. 30 stories in 7 sections — Europe, Ukraine, the Americas,
the Middle East & Africa, business & tech, science & health, and sport —
every one first reported on 22 November 2026.

- Entry image: `entry.png` (1024×1024, PNG, under 1 MiB), drawn by `draw.py`
  (authoring-time tool, Python + Pillow).
- Entrypoint: `main.ts` copies the committed `entry.png` to the output
  directory and verifies the copy. It never runs `draw.py`.
- Run: `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts`
